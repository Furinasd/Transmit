# Project Goal

Build a third-person 3D combat-puzzle vertical slice around **Transmit**.

## Core Promise

Players do not move objects directly. They read, capture, carry, transfer, and convert **Motion State** that already exists in the world.

The same motion grammar must create both combat decisions and mechanical puzzles across:

- Player
- Enemy
- Environment

## Success Criteria

- The first Capture / Transfer payoff occurs within 40 seconds.
- After seeing the interaction, a new player can explain within 10 seconds that motion moved from one actor to another—not the object itself.
- L1–L5 form an approximately 11.5-minute paper progression.
- L1–L3 form a continuous 5–7-minute playable demo.
- L1–L3 prove toy quality, directional reasoning, and threat reversal without introducing a second gameplay grammar.
- A five-person blind test records comprehension, voluntary reuse, prediction, friction, threat reframing, and completion time.
- Reset / Fail / Restart work from a clean build, with no developer-machine-only paths or uncommitted dependencies.
- The project remains compatible across supported UE 5.8 target platforms.

## Non-Goals

- Telekinesis, object grabbing, or arbitrary object throwing
- Player-generated motion or aim-based rewriting of motion direction
- Gunplay, DPS rotations, or health-bar clearing as the primary combat loop
- Separate combat and puzzle rule sets
- Deep skill trees, equipment progression, or a content-heavy enemy roster
- Strict Newtonian simulation when deterministic gameplay rules are clearer
- Art-asset-driven spectacle, tutorial-text dependence, or large narrative scope
