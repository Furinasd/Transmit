# L_Transmit v0.1 — spatial design and evidence

Experiment baseline: `feat/gameplay-core-v03` at `4db656b`. Work belongs only to `exp/astra-ltransmit-v01`. This is a candidate for human playtesting, not a release acceptance record.

## Inherited game and reconciled truth

The existing game is an ownership-based motion toy: E takes a Motion State, Q transfers it through one authoritative preview/commit path, and R restores the initial room snapshot. The player holds one state. Ordinary Linear uses CameraCanonical; Charger Dash authors PreserveSource High Motion. No health, attack, new motion type, or alternate manipulation system is needed.

`DESIGN_CONTRACT.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, and `STATE.md` still describe the `1fb96ea` checkpoint and claim Carrier, PreserveSource, and Actor Preview repair are absent. Verified commits `2a17171`, `a5166de`, and `d9b8c4a` implement them. `4db656b` stages 20 actors in L_TestChamber, including Carrier_CellB and Charger_CellC; README's absent-fixture prose also lags. Product rules remain authoritative; those status claims are historical. New production map, Ram, Gate, and encounter progression were actually absent.

The resolver emits the camera basis closest to incoming motion, not arbitrary aim steering. A horizontal 90-degree reroute cannot be assumed. Vertical input also dominates pitch selection, so a vertical-to-horizontal relay would require changing a frozen policy. The candidate uses a horizontal 30-degree reroute instead.

## Spatial thesis

One interrupted industrial transmission line changes meaning three times: a walkable deck, a remote payload, then a ram impact. Player travel and motion travel share visual continuity but diverge physically. The final exit is part of the same apparatus, rather than a separate combat reward.

The geometry is mechanically necessary: remove the first gap and the bridge is optional; remove the low throat and the player can carry the route resource directly; remove the diagonal throat and rerouting loses its purpose; remove the dash corridor and safe pockets and interception loses its spatial decision.

## Learn

A 9m break interrupts a 14m-wide approach. An amber source sits before a cyan 10m sliding bridge. Its deck begins retracted and advances along its own rail to a physical stop, spanning the break. Source and target are offset so the stopped source cannot occlude the first transfer. Near/far banks frame the outcome. The initial two controls are available as a small in-world legend; guidance thereafter is geometry, motion, and color.

## Route

The amber carrier enters a low mechanical culvert while the player follows an outer inspection gallery. Roof clearance admits the carrier and excludes the standing character. Partial sides expose moving amber through the structure. At the catch station a stable physical stop provides a generous recapture opportunity; a 30-degree throat then demands a second transfer from a new stance. Only physically delivering the still-owned ordinary Motion into the dock arms the Ram. Carrying the state directly to the next zone is insufficient.

## Weaponize

The armed linkage visually leads across the Charger lane to the Ram. Cover breaks the sightline and funnels crossing through an exposed gap. The Charger has one fixed +X dash axis, matching the Ram. Capturing preserves that committed direction even when turning toward the Ram. Two separate High Motion deliveries produce two visible impacts and open the only exit. Ordinary Motion cannot substitute.

The encounter is a repeatable authored lane, not a general AI opponent: no chase behavior, Behavior Tree, health, extra enemy, or free-direction retargeting. A global restart is retained for v0.1; deaths/falls restore the original ownership snapshot and all level progress.

## Evidence and rejected attempts

- Initial source-aligned macOS Editor build succeeded (target up to date).
- Five existing gameplay Blueprints compiled `BS_UP_TO_DATE` without saving inherited assets.
- All 16 `Transmit.MotionTransfer` tests passed on the inherited source.
- Native Editor console coordinate input was unreliable; the persistent authoring session uses `-ExecCmds` to run a temporary script at launch. `-ExecutePythonScript` is unsuitable for interactive PIE because its batch runner exits after the script completes.
- First scripted baseline transfer selected the stopped Source ahead of Carrier. Moving closer selected Carrier but the frozen animated Source body still occluded it. A perpendicular stance preserves +X output through camera-relative Left and removes the obstruction. This is a targeting/fixture-placement lesson, not a targeting-algorithm change.
- Baseline Charger reached x≈966.47 against BossTarget geometry. Subsequent cycles repeatedly went Telegraph→Recovery at the same position with no capture window. The level-specific Charger subclass therefore returns to its authored bay between cycles; the inherited Charger class remains unchanged.

## Cuts and acceptance boundaries

No environment art pass, checkpoint framework, moving camera sequence, second enemy, new Motion type, PCG, BeatMarker expansion, narrative layer, or packaging work. The 5–7 minute duration is a blind-play target, not something an automated teleport-assisted test can prove. Ely must judge whether the first payoff is understood within 40 seconds, the route is predicted rather than guessed, and threat is reinterpreted as resource.

Fresh candidate evidence and exact scope are recorded below as validation completes.

## Candidate iteration 1 — traversal evidence

The first continuous, non-teleport PIE run successfully captured the Learn Source and transferred to Bridge. It failed walking to x600: Player stopped at x144.62. The bridge had incorrectly reported an immediate collision stop: its swept box overlapped the Source plinth at y=-290, while its 70cm top exceeded character step height. Revision moves Source/plinth to y=-430 and gives the deck a 30cm thickness with a 40cm top. Validation now requires the bridge to reach x>1600 rather than treating any collision stop as success.

Independent geometry review identified a west-edge bypass around both arena baffles. Baffles now meet the west wall and rise to 560cm. Learn parapets are separated at the gap so their tops cannot serve as an alternative bridge. A code review also found a synchronous-reset latch bug in the new director: setting a fall latch after `RequestRoomReset` overwrote the reset callback and disabled later fall recovery. The level-specific director now requests reset directly when below the fall plane; core reset semantics remain unchanged.

- Live traversal crossed the revised bridge without teleporting. Route Capture/Transfer, outer-gallery pursuit and re-capture succeeded. A perpendicular end wall then blocked the re-routed +30° state immediately at x5379.04; a collision trace named `Route_CatchStop`.
- The catch wall is now tangent to the outgoing 30° rail. It stops the incoming +X carrier at x5472, then permits the re-routed state to leave along its face. The player approaches the visible west side of the stop for recapture; the new guide line terminates at that station. Actual Preview and Commit both recorded `(0.866025,0.5,0)` and the dock armed successfully.
- An initial arena approach left the floor and correctly triggered a full reset. A west connection revision collided with `Route_DiagonalCurb_-245`, proved by capsule trace. That connection was rejected. The final approach goes around the diagonal rail's southern end through a south-wall opening, preserving the baffles and compulsory lane crossing.
